**See a product. Feel the vibe. Break it down. Build your own.**

VibeCopy is a toolkit for turning SaaS inspiration into something you can actually build.

Discover products worth studying, understand what makes them work, analyze their features and competitors, and turn the whole thing into a clear, actionable plan. Instead of blindly copying what you see, VibeCopy helps you understand the *vibe* — the product, the experience, the mechanics, and the effort behind it.

Find something you like. Figure out what makes it tick. Decide what you can reproduce. Then build your own version.
